📌 Key Business Insights

1. Overall Performance

The hotel recorded 36K bookings and 8.39M in revenue across 59K nights, at an average price of 103.42 per room and an average lead time of about 85 days.

2. Booking Seasonality

October recorded the highest bookings (about 5.3K), while January and February were much lower. Revenue also peaked around September and October at about 1.17M a month.

3. High Cancellation Rate

The overall cancellation rate is 32.76% (12K of 36K bookings), a major risk to revenue.

4. Lead Time Risk

Bookings made 30+ days ahead cancel at 42%, while bookings made 0-3 days ahead cancel at only 7%.

5. Room Type Preference

Room Type 1 takes 28K bookings and 68.85% of revenue. Room Type 6 has the highest average price (182) and the highest cancellation rate (0.42), while Types 3, 5 and 7 have very low volumes.

6. Market Segments

Online is the biggest segment with 23K bookings and about 5.98M revenue (roughly 71%), followed by Offline (11K, 2.10M). Online also cancels the most (0.37), against 0.11 for Corporate.

7. Guest Behaviour

98.36% of guests are new and only 1.64% are repeat guests. Corporate and complementary bookings have the highest repeat rates, and most bookings have 2 adults (56K).

8. Parking and Special Requests

Guests who need car parking cancel far less (about 0.10 vs 0.33), and most bookings (36K) come with no special requests.

💡 Business Recommendations

Reduce Long-Lead-Time Cancellations

Use deposits, non-refundable rates or reconfirmation reminders for bookings made 30+ days in advance.

Improve Online Booking Retention

Offer prepaid discounts and reminders for online bookings, which have the highest cancellation rate.

Build Guest Loyalty

With only 1.64% repeat guests, introduce loyalty rewards and corporate partnerships to bring guests back.

Use Seasonal Pricing and Promotions

Push offers in low months (January and February) and keep premium pricing in August to October, when demand is highest.
