📌 Key Business Insights

1. Overall Sales Performance

The platform generated R$13.59M in revenue from about 99K orders and 96K customers.

2. Revenue Trend

Revenue stayed relatively stable in most months, with a noticeable peak around November 2017 at R$1.01M.

3. Top-Performing Categories

Watches & Gifts (R$1.21M), Sports & Leisure (R$0.99M) and Toys (R$0.48M) were the highest revenue categories.

4. Geographic Performance

São Paulo (SP) generated the most orders, making it the key market and a priority region for growth.

5. Payment Performance

Payment methods showed a relatively balanced distribution, with no single method dominating volume or revenue.

6. Order Fulfillment

About 97% of orders were successfully delivered, showing strong fulfillment performance.

7. Delivery Performance

Around 89% of orders arrived on time, 7% were late and 2% were not delivered.

8. Customer Retention

Only 3K repeat customers against 93K one-time customers, a big opportunity to improve retention.

9. Average Delivery Time

The average delivery time was about 12 days, but it varied a lot between states.

10. States with Longer Delivery Times

RR recorded about 29 days, followed by PA at 23 days and MA and SE at about 21 days. These regions need a logistics review.

💡 Business Recommendations

Improve Customer Retention

Introduce loyalty programs and personalized offers to increase repeat purchases.

Optimize Regional Delivery

Investigate logistics performance in RR, PA, MA and SE to reduce delivery times.

Focus on High-Performing Categories

Prioritize marketing and inventory for Watches & Gifts, Sports & Leisure, and Toys.

Reduce Late Deliveries

Identify the main causes of late orders and work with logistics partners to improve on-time delivery.
