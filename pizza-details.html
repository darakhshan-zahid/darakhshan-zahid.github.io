📌 Key Business Insights

1. Overall Performance

The business earned $340K from 8.9K orders and 20.7K pizzas, with an average order value of $38.4 and 2.33 pizzas per order.

2. Busiest Days

Thursday (1,433 orders) and Friday (1,427) are the busiest days. Sunday (1,063) and Monday (1,025) are the slowest, roughly 28% below Thursday.

3. Peak Hours

Orders peak twice a day: around 12-1 PM (about 1,050 orders an hour) and 5-6 PM (about 1,000). They fall steadily after 8 PM and almost stop by 11 PM.

4. Category Performance

Classic pizzas lead with 29.52% of sales and about 6K pizzas sold. Supreme (24.13%), Veggie (23.48%) and Chicken (22.87%) are fairly balanced.

5. Size Performance

Large pizzas bring 36.08% of sales, followed by Medium (31.85%) and Small (30.55%). XL (1.44%) and XXL are almost negligible.

6. Best Sellers

The Classic Deluxe (1,066), The Hawaiian (1,045) and The Pepperoni (1,019) are the top sellers by pizzas sold.

7. Worst Seller

The Brie Carre Pizza sold only 206, about half of the next-lowest pizza (The Calabrese, 388).

8. Order Basket

Customers buy 2.33 pizzas per order on average, which leaves room to grow order value with combos and bundles.

💡 Business Recommendations

Staff and Stock for Peak Hours

Schedule more staff and prepare ingredients for the 12-1 PM and 5-6 PM rushes, especially on Thursdays and Fridays.

Boost Slow Days

Use combo deals and offers on Sundays and Mondays to lift order volume.

Promote Top Sellers, Review the Weakest

Feature Classic Deluxe, Hawaiian and Pepperoni in campaigns, and reconsider or reposition The Brie Carre Pizza.

Increase Basket Size

Introduce bundles and family deals to raise pizzas per order, and simplify the menu by reviewing XL and XXL sizes.

⚙️ Code & Technical Implementation

create database pizza_sales;

# A. KPI's
#1. Total Revenue
select sum(total_price) as total_revenue from pizza_sales.data;

#2. Average Order Value
select sum(total_price) / count(distinct order_id) as Average_Order_Value from pizza_sales.data;

#3. Total Pizza Sold
select sum(quantity) as Total_Pizza_Sold from pizza_sales.data;

#4. Total Orders
select count(distinct order_id) as Total_Orders from pizza_sales.data;

#5. Average Pizzas Per Order
select cast(
cast(sum(quantity) as decimal(10, 2)) /
cast(count(distinct order_id) as decimal(10, 2)) as decimal(10, 2)
) as Average_pizza_per_order
from pizza_sales.data;

#B. Daily Trend For Total Orders
SELECT
    DAYNAME(STR_TO_DATE(order_date, '%m/%d/%Y')) AS order_day,
    COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS total_orders
FROM pizza_sales.data
GROUP BY DAYNAME(STR_TO_DATE(order_date, '%m/%d/%Y'));

#C. Hourly Trend for Orders
select hour(order_time) as order_hours,
count(distinct order_id) as total_orders
from pizza_sales.data
group by hour(order_time)
order by hour(order_time);

#D. % of sales by Pizza Category
select pizza_category, cast(sum(total_price) as decimal(10,2)) as total_revenue,
cast(sum(total_price) * 100 / (select sum(total_price) from pizza_sales.data)
as decimal(10,2)) as PCT from pizza_sales.data
group by pizza_category;

#E. % of sales by pizza size
select pizza_size, cast(sum(total_price) as decimal(10,2)) as total_revenue,
cast(sum(total_price) * 100 / (select sum(total_price) from pizza_sales.data)
as decimal(10,2)) as PCT from pizza_sales.data
group by pizza_size
order by pizza_size;

#F. Total Pizza sold By Pizza Category
SELECT pizza_category, SUM(quantity) as Total_Quantity_Sold
FROM pizza_sales.data
GROUP BY pizza_category
ORDER BY Total_Quantity_Sold DESC;

#G. Top 5 Best Sellers by Total Pizzas Sold
select pizza_name, sum(quantity) as Total_Pizza_Sold
from pizza_sales.data
group by pizza_name
order by Total_Pizza_Sold desc limit 5;

#H. Bottom 5 Sellers by Total Pizzas Sold
select pizza_name, sum(quantity) as Total_Pizza_Sold
from pizza_sales.data
group by pizza_name
order by Total_Pizza_Sold Asc limit 5;
