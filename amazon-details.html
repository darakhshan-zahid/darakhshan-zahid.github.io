📌 Key Business Insights

1. Overall Performance

From 2020 to 2024 the business handled 100K orders from 43K customers, selling 300K units and generating 91.83M in revenue through 2K sellers.

2. Revenue Trend

Monthly revenue stayed in a tight band of about 1.34M to 1.64M across all five years, showing stable demand but little sustained growth.

3. Category Performance

All six categories are close: Electronics leads at 15.6M, while Sports & Outdoors, Books, Clothing, Toys & Games and Home & Kitchen each earn between 15.2M and 15.3M.

4. Geographic Concentration

The United States generates 70K of the 100K orders (70%), followed by India (15K), Canada (6K), the United Kingdom (5K) and Australia (4K).

5. Payment Methods

Credit Card brings 34.98% of revenue and Debit Card 20.19%. UPI (15.13%) and Amazon Pay (14.92%) follow, while Cash on Delivery is only 4.92%.

6. Order Status

75K orders were delivered, 15K are shipped, 4K pending, and 3K returned and 3K cancelled (about 6% combined).

7. Discount Impact

43.3% of revenue came from orders with no discount, while the rest came from discount levels between 5% and 30%.

8. Customer Value

On average each customer placed about 2.3 orders, and the average order value works out to roughly 918 (calculated from the dashboard totals).

💡 Business Recommendations

Grow Beyond the US Market

India, Canada, the UK and Australia have far fewer orders than the US, so targeted marketing there could add growth.

Reduce Returns, Cancellations and Pending Orders

Find the main causes and improve product listings, stock checks and fulfilment speed.

Use Targeted Discounts

Since 43% of revenue already comes from full-price orders, test selective offers instead of broad discounts to protect margins.

Break the Revenue Plateau

Use bundles, cross-selling and seasonal campaigns across the six evenly performing categories to lift monthly revenue above its current range.
